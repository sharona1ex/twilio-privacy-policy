# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [9/1/2026]

Sharon and Stephy we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information in connection with our wedding communications, including wedding RSVP information and text-message updates.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following information about invited wedding guests:

- Name
- Mobile phone number
- Email address, if voluntarily provided
- Mailing address, if voluntarily provided
- Wedding RSVP response, including attendance status, guest count, meal selection, dietary needs, and other event-related preferences
- Messages or replies sent to us regarding the wedding

## How We Collect Phone Numbers

Mobile phone numbers used for wedding communications are collected directly from friends, relatives, and invited guests through existing personal relationships and contact information voluntarily shared with us. Numbers may also be provided directly by guests through conversations, phone calls, email, wedding-related communications, RSVP interactions, or an RSVP/wedding website form.

We use mobile phone numbers only to send limited, non-commercial wedding-related messages, such as:

- Wedding invitations and RSVP links
- RSVP reminders
- Event date, time, venue, travel, parking, or schedule updates
- Time-sensitive wedding-day logistics

We do not buy, rent, scrape, or obtain phone numbers from third-party marketing lists.

## Text Message Consent

By providing a mobile phone number directly to us for wedding-related communication, or by responding affirmatively to a request to receive wedding text updates, a guest agrees to receive limited informational text messages regarding our wedding.

Message frequency varies, but we expect to send no more than approximately three to five wedding-related messages before the wedding, except when a guest directly requests additional information or a time-sensitive event update is necessary.

Message and data rates may apply.

Guests may opt out of text messages at any time by replying **STOP**. For help, reply **HELP** or contact us at [contact email address].

## How We Use Information

We use personal information only to:

- Maintain the wedding guest list
- Send wedding invitations, RSVP links, reminders, and event updates
- Track RSVP responses and guest preferences
- Communicate directly with guests regarding the wedding
- Plan and coordinate wedding-related events

We will not use guest information for unrelated marketing, sales, advertising, or commercial purposes.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

**Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes.**

We may use service providers solely to help us operate wedding communications and RSVP tools, such as our wedding website provider, RSVP platform, spreadsheet/storage provider, or text-message delivery provider. Those providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to us.

## Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect guest information, including limiting access to the guest list and using reputable service providers.

We retain wedding guest information only as long as reasonably necessary to coordinate our wedding and related communications. After the wedding, we will delete or securely limit access to the guest list and RSVP records when they are no longer needed, except where we need to keep limited records for personal planning or legal purposes.
