These SMS Terms of Service (“SMS Terms”) govern the Sharon & Stephy Wedding RSVP Updates text messaging program (“Program”). By opting in to receive text messages from Sharon and Stephy, you agree to these SMS Terms.

## Program Description

The Program provides non-commercial, wedding-related messages from Sharon and Stephy to invited wedding guests. Messages may include:

- A link to RSVP for the wedding
- RSVP deadline reminders
- Limited wedding-event updates directly related to RSVP coordination

The wedding is scheduled for November 21, 2026, at Prestonwood Baptist Church, Plano Campus.

## How to Opt In

Guests may opt in by replying **YES** to RSVP request from Sharon and Stephy. The consent request explains that the guest may receive wedding RSVP messages, including an RSVP link and up to two RSVP reminders.

Only guests who affirmatively reply **YES** are added to the Program’s automated SMS list. Consent to receive text messages is optional. Guests can receive wedding information through the WithJoy wedding website, email, WhatsApp, phone call, or other personal communication without opting in to SMS.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies, but participants should expect to receive **up to three messages total before the wedding**: one RSVP-link message and up to two RSVP-deadline reminders.

Additional messages may be sent only in response to a guest’s direct question or when necessary to communicate a critical wedding-related update.

## Opting Out

You may opt out of the Program at any time by replying **STOP** to any message from Sharon and Stephy. After you send STOP, you may receive one final text confirming that you have been unsubscribed. After confirmation, you will receive no further automated wedding RSVP messages from this Program unless you separately opt in again.
